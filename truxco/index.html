About Truxco

A Swedish bus partner for all of Europe.

Founded in Lund and run by owners Malika and Matin Haidari, Truxco has grown into one of the leading used bus dealers in the Nordics. From our base in Staffanstorp we deliver hundreds of buses every year to operators across Europe.

  • Honest, transparent deals: clear condition reports and fixed prices.
  • Pan-European logistics: we handle export, transport and paperwork.
  • Personal service: one contact person from first call to delivery.
