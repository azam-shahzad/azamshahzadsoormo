EQ logo options

Each direction keeps EQ as the main mark and enrouteQ as the tagline, in a deeper ember orange and emerald green taken from the current logo. Every option is shown on white, on dark, and at small icon sizes. Round 2 is new: simpler marks about energy and navigation, using one or two flat colours.
