Each direction keeps EQ as the main mark and enrouteQ as the tagline, in a deeper ember orange and emerald green taken from the current logo. Every option is shown on white, on dark, and at small icon sizes. Round 7 is new: option 42 (Speed) reworked, with the Q in a black circle and the E shown only as its three lines.
