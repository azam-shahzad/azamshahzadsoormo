Quick answers

How quickly will you reply?

We reply to emails within 24 hours, and a specialist gets back to every form request within one business day.

Can I see a bus before I buy it?

Yes. Visits to our yard in Staffanstorp are by appointment only. Choose “Visit the yard” in the form or call us to book a time.

Do you deliver buses outside Sweden?

Yes. We serve operators across Europe and help with transport and export papers.

Our team speaks Swedish, English, German and Polish.
