Our story

We are Truxco.

We started in Lund in 2015 with one idea: bus trading should be personal and direct.

Today we are a growing team based in Staffanstorp, with an extra office in Lund. As part of the family-owned Laryan Holding group, we trade new and used vehicles — from a single bus to a full fleet.
