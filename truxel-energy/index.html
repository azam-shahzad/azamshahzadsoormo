FAQ

Questions from control rooms.

Can't find your answer? Our grid engineers reply within one working day.

Do we need to replace any hardware?

No. Truxel connects to your existing SCADA, EMS and meters through standard protocols. Most sites need only a gateway VM inside the OT network.

Who stays in control of dispatch?

Your operators. Truxel runs in recommendation mode until you approve automation, and every automated action stays inside limits you define.

How is our operational data secured?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, OT connections are outbound-only, and deployments follow IEC 62443. On-premise hosting is available.

Which markets do you support?

Day-ahead, intraday and balancing markets, plus capacity and frequency-response products in most regulated and liberalised power markets.

How is pricing structured?

An annual platform fee based on managed capacity in MW. Many customers choose a shared-savings model for the first year.
